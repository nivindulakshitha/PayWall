💰 FEE CALCULATION
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

Formula: Base Fee + Distance + Frequency Surcharge - Discount + Fuel

Base Fees:
  Grade 6-9: Rs. 2,500
  O/L: Rs. 3,500
  A/L: Rs. 5,000

Adjustments:
  • Distance: (input + 8km) × Rs.30/km × sessions/month
  • Frequency: +10% per extra session
  • Group: -5% (2 students) or -10% (3+)
  • Fuel: Same as distance (physical only)
  • Online: Ignores distance/fuel
