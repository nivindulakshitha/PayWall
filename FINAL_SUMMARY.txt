💰 FEE FORMULA DETAILS
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

BASE FEES (Monthly)
  Grade 6-9:     Rs. 2,500
  O/L (10-11):   Rs. 3,500
  A/L (12-13):   Rs. 5,000

DISTANCE CALCULATION
  • User enters distance from Wariyapola
  • System adds 8 km constant
  • Total Distance = User Input + 8
  • Charge: Rs. 30/km per session
  • Applied ONLY for physical classes
  • Calculation: (User Distance + 8) × 30 × (Frequency × 4.33)

FREQUENCY SURCHARGE
  1x/week:  No surcharge
  2x/week:  +10% of base fee (1 extra session)
  3x/week:  +20% of base fee (2 extra sessions)

GROUP DISCOUNT
  1 student:  0% (no discount)
  2 students: -5%
  3+ students: -10%

FUEL CHARGE
  • Same as distance surcharge
  • Physical only (Online: Rs. 0)
  • Calculation: (User Distance + 8) × 30 × (Frequency × 4.33)

FINAL CALCULATION
  Monthly Fee = Base Fee + Distance Surcharge + Frequency Surcharge
                - Group Discount + Fuel Charge
              → ROUNDED to nearest Rs. 500

  Per-Session Fee = Monthly Fee ÷ (Frequency × 4.33 weeks/month)

EXAMPLE
  Input: O/L, 5km from Wariyapola, Physical, 2x/week, 2 students
  
  • Base: Rs. 3,500
  • Distance: (5+8) × 30 × (2 × 4.33) = Rs. 3,385.80
  • Frequency: 3,500 × 10% = Rs. 350
  • Subtotal: 3,500 + 3,385.80 + 350 = Rs. 7,235.80
  • Discount: 7,235.80 × 5% = Rs. 361.79
  • Fuel: (5+8) × 30 × (2 × 4.33) = Rs. 3,385.80
  • Total: 7,235.80 - 361.79 + 3,385.80 = Rs. 10,259.81
  • ROUNDED: Rs. 10,500 (monthly)
  • Per-Session: Rs. 10,500 ÷ (2 × 4.33) = Rs. 1,210
