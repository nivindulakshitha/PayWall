💰 FEE CALCULATION FORMULA
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

BASE FEES (Monthly)
  Grade 6-9:     Rs. 2,500
  O/L (10-11):   Rs. 3,500
  A/L (12-13):   Rs. 5,000

ADJUSTMENTS
  Distance Surcharge:
    • User Distance + 8 km constant
    • Rs. 30/km per session
    • Physical only (Online: Rs. 0)

  Frequency Surcharge:
    • 1x/week:  No surcharge
    • 2x/week:  +10% of base fee
    • 3x/week:  +20% of base fee

  Group Discount:
    • 1 student:  0%
    • 2 students: -5%
    • 3+ students: -10%

  Fuel Charge:
    • Rs. 30/km per session
    • Physical only (Online: Rs. 0)

CALCULATION
  Monthly Fee = Base Fee + Distance Surcharge + Frequency Surcharge 
                - Group Discount + Fuel Charge
              → Rounded to nearest Rs. 500

  Per-Session Fee = Monthly Fee / (Frequency × 4.33)


🌟 EXAMPLE CALCULATION
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

Input:
  • Grade: O/L (Rs. 3,500)
  • Distance: 5 km → Total: 5 + 8 = 13 km
  • Method: Physical
  • Frequency: 2x/week (1 extra session)
  • Students: 2

Calculation:
  1. Base Fee: 3,500
  2. Distance: 13 × 30 × (2 × 4.33) = 3,385.80
  3. Frequency: 3,500 × 10% = 350
  4. Subtotal: 3,500 + 3,385.80 + 350 = 7,235.80
  5. Discount: 7,235.80 × 5% = 361.79
  6. Fuel: 13 × 30 × (2 × 4.33) = 3,385.80
  7. Total: 7,235.80 - 361.79 + 3,385.80 = 10,259.81
  8. Rounded: Rs. 10,500 (Monthly)
  9. Per-Session: 10,500 / (2 × 4.33) ≈ Rs. 1,210
