FEE CALCULATION FORMULA
════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

Monthly Fee = Base Fee 
            + Distance Surcharge (if physical)
            + Frequency Surcharge
            - Group Discount
            + Fuel Charge (if physical)
            → Rounded to Rs. 500

Base Fees:
  Grade 6-9:     Rs. 2,500
  O/L (10-11):   Rs. 3,500
  A/L (12-13):   Rs. 5,000

Adjustments:
  • Distance: (Input + 8km) × Rs. 30/km × (Frequency × 4.33 weeks/month)
  • Frequency: +10% per extra session (beyond 1x/week)
  • Group: -5% (2 students), -10% (3+ students)
  • Fuel: Same calculation as distance (physical only)
  • Online: Ignores all distance charges

EXAMPLE CALCULATION
════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

Input:
  Grade: O/L (Rs. 3,500)
  Distance: 5km (+ 8km = 13km total)
  Method: Physical
  Frequency: 2x/week (1 extra = 10% surcharge)
  Students: 2 (= 5% discount)

Calculation:
  Base Fee:           Rs. 3,500.00
  Distance Surcharge: 13 × 30 × (2 × 4.33) = Rs. 3,385.80
  Frequency:          3,500 × 10% = Rs. 350.00
  Subtotal:           Rs. 7,235.80
  Group Discount:     7,235.80 × 5% = Rs. -361.79
  Fuel Charge:        13 × 30 × (2 × 4.33) = Rs. 3,385.80
  ─────────────────────────────────────────
  TOTAL:              Rs. 10,259.81
  ROUNDED:            Rs. 10,500 ← Monthly Fee
  Per-Session:        Rs. 10,500 ÷ (2 × 4.33) = Rs. 1,210
